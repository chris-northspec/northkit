### Sprint 3: visual v2, check-out and in, transfers, who has what, stocktake, offline (branch `sprint-3/movements`)
- **Chris's answers (S3-0):** K31 approved; K30 stays with Chris; **K22 WebP thumbnails built**: `webp-wasm` (MIT; libwebp as WebAssembly from Squoosh, Apache-2.0) decodes WebP in the worker, with a test that a WebP upload gets a 320 px thumbnail and that one the worker can't decode is marked as having none. **The `demo-link.spec.ts` flake:** traced to `networkidle` waits; under the development server the hot-reload connection keeps the network busy, so the wait timed out, or returned early and a click submitted the plain form before React had taken it over. Uplifted: the shell (grouped sections, icons, collapsing to icons on a narrow desktop), the overview (four stat cards from real movement history with a sparkline, delta chips in words and footer links to lists with matching counts; the depots table beside a needs-action panel with severity edges; the all-clear picture), the item list (summary strip, identity column, where it is, due back), the item page (a hero "where it is now" card and the movement timeline), kinds, import, settings, sign-in and the scan page. Loading, empty and error states designed; never a full-page spinner. `contrast.test.ts` covers every new pair in light and dark; `tokens.test.ts` fails on any hard-coded radius or shadow as it does on a colour. Before and after screenshots in `docs/screens/visual-v2/`.
- **Check out and in (S3-2, A10):** migration `0006_movements`: the append-only `movements.movements` record (never updated or deleted, by the app or the owner; partitioned by month from six months back), the `register.items` whereabouts guard (position, holder, due-back date, depot and the statuses out, in transit and missing change only inside the movement trigger, K36), `in_transit` as a status, kinds' `default_days_out` (K33), vans' "looked after by" person and locations' last-counted date. The owner-run movement trigger enforces: only an available item goes out and the refusal says why (out, quarantined, missing, in transit, retired); a kit moves its parts in the same statement and a part out on its own blocks the kit by name; a check-in needs an item that is out; an item is checked out once under two phones at the same moment. Services for check-out (one or many, to me, my van, a site, a store or a person), check-in (the depot's store by default, with a condition note), report missing and found, with the roles in K37. Phone flows at `/move/out`, `/move/in`, `/move/missing`: one step per screen, a camera scanner (the browser's BarcodeDetector where it has one, jsQR otherwise; no frame leaves the phone) or a typed asset number, a due-back date offered from the kind.
- **Transfers (S3-3, A18):** Send (in transit with the sender, carrier and expected date) then Receive at the destination by scan or from `/transfers`; an item in transit can't be checked out; a transfer past its date needs action at both depots; both depots see the transfer and neither sees the other's other items (an RLS policy on either depot, and an isolation test); the item's home depot changes on receipt, photos follow (K35); the sender can take it back.
- **Who has what (S3-4):** by person, van, site and store with counts, overdue returns and a CSV; My kit; overdue returns on the overview card, the person's page and in needs action; `overdue_at` stored for Sprint 4's reminders.
- **Stocktake (S3-5, A17):** start a count of a store, cage, yard or van (one at a time per location), scan what is there, then the result: expected and found; expected but not found, each marked Missing or Ignored with a reason; found but not expected, each Move here or Note only. Closing records the result, applies only the choices made, is audited and never changes again; each location keeps its last-counted date and ones past `stocktake.recount_days` (default 30, K34) need action.
- **Offline (S3-6, ADR 0004, A16):** NorthKeep's offline runner pattern copied and trimmed (service worker for the phone screens, an IndexedDB queue per person with client ids, replay in order with back-off, a banner with the count, a "Changes not sent yet" page): the phone keeps a copy of the kit it may see with label codes, so scanning works with no signal; one `/api/sync` endpoint applies a replay once. **The server is the record of truth:** a change that no longer fits is refused with the reason, kept and shown, never applied silently (K38). Covers check-out, check-in, bulk check-out, report missing and stocktake scans. `offline.spec.ts`: five movements and a stocktake offline, the phone closed and reopened, synced; a clash; **20 of 20 runs passed**.
- **Demo (S3-7):** `pnpm db:seed-demo` adds three months of history through the same services the screens use: 750 planned movements, 12 items overdue, 3 missing, 2 transfers in transit (one late), a stocktake on each depot's store with differences; the overview sparkline shows it; running it twice gives the same result. `docs/screens/` retaken at 1280 and 390 × 844.

### Sprint 2: items, kinds, labels, photos, import (branch `sprint-2/items`)
- **Kinds and rules (S2-1):** eight starter kinds with editable inspection rules (type, interval, required, a note) taken from `docs/research/compliance-sources.md`; every screen says they are your own schedule settings, not legal advice. A kind in use can't be deleted (the database refuses); deactivate instead.

Kit sets one level deep. Status transitions, kits, hire details and retire reasons are enforced in the database. List with search, filters (kind, depot, status, owned or hired in, kits) and paging; CSV of the filtered list; history from the audit log; retire with a reason.
