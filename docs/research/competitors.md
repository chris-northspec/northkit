## What this says about the gap (to test with buyers)

1. **Confirmed on what we read:** among UK-relevant products that handle LOLER and PUWER, none offers self-hosting or a one-off licence.
2. **Confirmed:** the inspection-firm tools (Lolerflow, CertFlow) serve the examiner; brand-tied tools (Hilti, Milwaukee) serve single-brand fleets.
3. **Not confirmed (a risk):** EquipReg targets the same owner, UK, LOLER and PUWER buyer and is not yet priced. Watch it.
4. **Price anchors** for the owner's register: €39 to €119 a month (Tooltagger, small kits), £33 to £249 a month (itemit), £1 per asset per week (CheckedSafe: about £2,600 a year for 50 assets, and £26,000 for 500), £250 a month for an inspector's tool (Lolerflow). A per-asset price gets expensive for a 600-item contractor, which supports a flat or one-off licence.
